Why quit?
Jab bhi man hile, yeh list padho.
Health
Agle 24 ghante me hi BP aur heart par kaafi load kam ho jata hai. Har din tobacco se door = heart attack ka risk kam.
Money
Jo paisa aaj bach raha hai, wahi future me aapke family ke kaam aayega, hospital ke bill ke nahi.
Family
Aapka ek decision – baccho ki sehat, partner ki tension, sab par direct effect. Wo khush chehra imagine karo jab aap permanent quit ho jaoge.
Self-respect
Har baar jab aap urge ko hara dete ho, aapka self-respect, confidence, willpower teenon grow karte hain. Ye sirf tobacco nahi, life ke har area me help karega.
Tobacco ke andar kya hota hai?
Ye "maza" nahi, chemicals ka mix hai.
Chemicals (simplified):
  • Nicotine: brain ko hijack karke addiction banata hai, BP & heart rate badhata hai.
  • Areca nut (supari): mouth ke andar fibrosis, ulcers aur cancer ka risk.
  • TSNAs: (tobacco-specific nitrosamines) – direct cancer causing compounds.
  • Tar: lungs aur airways me chipak kar damage karta hai.
  • Cadmium: kidney damage, bones kamzor.
  • Lead: nerves & brain function disturb, BP par effect.
  • Carbon monoxide: blood me oxygen kam, thakan aur heart load zyada.

Jab bhi "thoda sa hi to hai" socho, yaad rakho ye thoda-thoda poison hi jama ho raha hai.

Body me kya hota hai?
Har packet / pouch = multiple organs ko damage.
  • Muh & gums: zakhm, infection, bad smell, white / red patches, oral cancer risk.
  • Brain: anxiety, irritability, focus kam – nicotine ke bina brain disturb lagta hai.
  • Heart & BP: BP high, heart attack & stroke ka risk jump.
  • Lungs: oxygen kam, breath short, stamina kam.
  • Stomach & pancreas: acidity, ulcers, pancreatic cancer risk.
  • Fertility: sperm quality / count me problem, pregnancy risks.
  • Skin & aging: jald hi budhape jaisa look, dull & dry skin.
  • Stress: asli stress badhata hai, sirf temporary relief ka illusion deta hai.

Good news: jitna jaldi quit karoge, utna zyada damage reverse ho sakta hai. Aaj se start = best investment.
